# Duty Mechanic verification

These terms keep Domestic's dungeon verification results comparable and distinguish mechanic responses from incidental NPC movement.

## Language

**Boss vulnerability allowance**:
At most one vulnerability stack gained during each boss encounter in a qualifying dungeon run. Each boss has its own allowance.
_Avoid_: One stack per dungeon

**Verification role**:
One of the four coverage groups selected for this campaign: tank, healer, melee DPS, or caster DPS. Physical ranged DPS is outside this campaign's certified coverage.
_Avoid_: Every combat role

**Mechanic-based positioning**:
Positioning derived from the mechanic's hazards, targets, participants, or required objects. Joining a marked party member or bringing a self-targeted stack to participants belongs to this category.
_Avoid_: NPC following when describing required stack participation

**NPC following**:
Using an NPC's movement or location as a proxy for safety rather than deriving the response from the mechanic itself.
_Avoid_: Mechanic-based positioning when merely tracking an NPC
